# Wear Your Batik: submission video prompt

Target length: 1 minute 55 seconds, leaving five seconds below the two-minute limit. Timing is estimated; rehearse once before exporting.

## Paste-ready production prompt

Create a 115-second hackathon submission demo video for **Wear Your Batik**, by Haziq Halifi and Timothy Lee. Use actual screen recordings and supplied project images as the proof. Make the video warm, clear and visually focused on the patterned cloth, garment preview and personal reveal. Use 16:9 at 1920 × 1080, readable English subtitles, restrained transitions and quiet instrumental music beneath the narration. Use cream and indigo title cards with botanical accents. Keep text readable on a laptop.

Use these seven scenes, with the narration below. Include short pauses for viewers to see each interaction. Show the latest local prototype for the 3D sequence. For try-on, show the supplied recorded synthetic-person result with its matching original source garment; label it “Recorded AI try-on · Synthetic demo person.” Do not present that result as generated from the newly customised design shown earlier. Do not fabricate interface screens, interactions, testimonials or outputs. If a clip is missing, leave a marked placeholder for the editor. Omit browser chrome, credentials, contact emails and developer consoles.
